## Deliverable SOW bullets

### Your SIGNAL DNA Assessment (Know)
- Full guided assessment
- Complete review of every response by your coach personally

### Your SIGNAL DNA Report (Know)
- A written Your SIGNAL DNA report, yours to keep
- Four career paths, each with the reasoning for fit, supporting evidence from your own background, the primary tradeoff, and what remains untested
- Realistic early-career entry points identified for each path
- Side-by-side comparison of all four paths against your own priority conditions
- Links from each path into the WRN Decoder library

### Your SIGNAL DNA Decode and Career Path Session (Know)
- Live 1-on-1 decode session walking through the full report

### Resume Workshop (Build)
- Live 1-on-1 resume workshop

### Resume (Build)
- Full resume rebuild, written line by line around your strengths, interests, and qualifications, with multiple versions as needed
- ATS-friendly structure

### Cover Letter (Build)
- A custom cover letter system written in your own voice, plus a reusable framework for future applications

### LinkedIn Rebuild (Build)
- LinkedIn positioning audit covering headline, summary, experience, and overall positioning

### SIGNAL Setup and Job Search Strategy (Search)
- SIGNAL job strategy and decision system training
- Clear rules for which postings to pursue and which to ignore
- Application prioritization and sequencing
- Application tracking through SIGNAL

### Networking Campaign (Search)
- A curated list of 100 to 300 targeted professionals aligned to your industry, role, and market
- A structured three-touch outreach campaign
- We build your target companies, contacts, and proposed messaging behind the scenes. You send the campaign from your own email account, so contacts interact with you directly

### Interview Sessions 1 to 3 (Land)
- Comprehensive interview strategy sessions
- AI interview practice assigned between sessions

### Mock Interview (Land)
- Recorded mock interview under realistic conditions
- Detailed evaluation and structured scorecard covering both content and delivery

### Pre-Interview Prep (Land)
- Live pre-interview preparation

## Phase closing notes

- Know: This is not a personality test. Nothing is scored or typed. Your coach reads every answer, and the report is built from your own examples in your own language. It narrows to four paths. It does not choose the career for you. Everything that follows is aimed using what we learn here.
- Build: This is 1-on-1 coaching, not a templated or AI-generated resume. Your materials are built around you specifically.
- Search: A substantial portion of this stage is behind-the-scenes work, list building and messaging, rather than live session time.
- Land: This stage includes up to 8 hours of live interview support.
