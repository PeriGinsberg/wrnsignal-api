# 00 · Methodology principles

**Status:** REVIEW
**Methodology version:** v0.8
**Owner:** Peri Ginsberg
**Last updated:** 2026-08-23

These are the governing principles. Every other document in this directory inherits them,
and any rule elsewhere that contradicts one of these is wrong, not an exception.

Principles P1–P7 are the initiative-level principles already recorded on the SIGNAL PM
initiative charter (`CNL — Initiative Charter: SIGNAL Career Navigation Loop`). P8–P12 are
methodology-specific and are stated here for the first time. Both sets are **REVIEW** —
written down for Peri to approve, not approved.

---

## P1 · Discover first. Contextualize second. Recommend third.

Professional DNA is calculated **blind**, from assessment responses only.

Resume, LinkedIn, coach notes, SIGNAL profile data, education, job history, stated career
goals and previously recommended paths must **not** influence the initial DNA calculation.

Context may be introduced **after** the DNA result is frozen — for interpretation,
validation, trajectory, positioning and downstream career navigation. Never before.

The full three-stage boundary is defined in [09-context-boundary.md](09-context-boundary.md).
That document is the operative one; this principle is why it exists.

## P2 · DNA informs the profile. It does not overwrite stated preferences.

SIGNAL preserves four distinct things and never collapses them:

1. What the client **says** they want.
2. What their **DNA** suggests may fit.
3. What their **evidence** supports.
4. What the **market** offers.

Where these disagree, the disagreement is the coaching information. It is recorded and
surfaced. It is never silently reconciled, averaged, or resolved in favour of whichever
source is most convenient.

## P3 · Career paths are hypotheses, not prescriptions.

Up to four paths. No forced single lane. The student's response to each path is one of
**PURSUE · TEST · KEEP OPEN · NO**.

A career trajectory is **not a job-title matching exercise**. Producing a list of titles
that pattern-match a profile is the failure mode this methodology exists to avoid.

## P4 · No personality trait is inherently superior.

Leadership, ambition, autonomy, extroversion and risk-taking are never framed as better
than support, expertise, stability, partnership, predictability or non-managerial
contribution.

Every construct is expressed with both what it gives and what it costs. A pole of a
continuum is never described in language that reads as the correct end.

## P5 · Capability is not preference. Preference is not capability.

Being good at something does not mean the person should pursue it. Wanting to do something
does not mean they can currently do it.

These are two separate measurements with two separate evidence bases, and the methodology
must be able to say "you are good at this and do not want it" without contradiction.

## P6 · Do not infer what cannot be known.

Work style, mentorship quality, culture, work-life boundaries, manager quality and team
dynamics cannot be established from a job posting. Where a posting does not establish
something, it becomes a **research question**, not a scored fact.

The same applies inside the assessment: where responses do not establish a construct, the
methodology says so through confidence rather than producing a number that looks certain.

## P7 · Build in releasable chunks.

Each production release creates immediate value for coached clients and lays infrastructure
for the next. This is a delivery principle, carried here because it constrains how much
methodology has to be frozen before R1 can start.

---

## P8 · Assessment length is earned by uncertainty.

Do not ask a question because it exists. Ask it because the answer is not yet determined
and the construct it serves has not reached its confidence requirement.

A shorter assessment that reaches confidence is better than a longer one that reaches the
same confidence. **Adaptive stopping is part of the methodology, not an optimisation
applied to it.**

See [03-assessment-architecture.md](03-assessment-architecture.md) and
[05-adaptive-clarifiers.md](05-adaptive-clarifiers.md).

## P9 · Contradictions trigger clarification, not averaging.

When two responses point in opposite directions on the same construct, the methodology
asks a clarifying question. It does not take the midpoint, drop the outlier, or average.

An unresolved contradiction is reported as **low confidence on that construct**, with the
contradiction visible — not smoothed into a confident-looking middle value.

## P10 · Derived insights require evidence, not eloquence.

A decoded insight appears only when sufficient evidence exists across every underlying
construct it draws on. Decoded insights are **not independent traits** and must never be
generated because the phrasing sounds compelling.

If the constructs underneath an insight are low-confidence, the insight does not appear.
See [06-decoded-insights.md](06-decoded-insights.md).

## P11 · The frozen artifact is immutable.

Once a Professional DNA result is frozen it never changes. A correction, a methodology
change, or new information produces a **new version**; the prior version stays readable.

A client's disagreement with an insight is recorded against the artifact. It does not edit
the artifact. (This follows from P2 — the disagreement is information, not an error to
correct.)

## P12 · Every generated statement is traceable to its evidence.

Every construct score, every decoded insight and every growth-edge statement carries
provenance back to the specific responses that produced it.

A statement that cannot be traced is **dropped, not softened**. Rewording an ungrounded
claim into something vaguer is worse than omitting it, because it survives review.
