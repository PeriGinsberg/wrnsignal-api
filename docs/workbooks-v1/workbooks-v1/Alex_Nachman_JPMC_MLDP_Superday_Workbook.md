## Section 2. Superday Game Plan

**What we know about this Superday**
- Two 30-minute Zoom interviews, back to back, with different interviewers.
- One MLDP candidate on Glassdoor reported a virtual Superday of two 25-minute interviews with members of the marketing team, said the questions were wordy and some were complicated, and said interviewers left a good amount of time for candidate questions.

**Rules for Monday**
1. **Same Alex, both rooms.** Your TMAY and your "why JPMorganChase" stay the same in both interviews. Assume the two interviewers compare notes.
2. **Different stories, both rooms.** Use different behavioral stories with each interviewer so the combined picture is bigger. Map this in Section 6.
3. **Wordy question? Restate it.** "Just to make sure I answer the right thing, you're asking about..." Then answer.
4. **Say less, let them ask.** 30 minutes goes fast. Target 60 to 90 seconds per answer. If they want more, they will ask.
5. **Save 8 to 10 minutes for your questions.** Plan for it.
6. **Each call is a fresh start.** Interviewer 2 does not know how Interviewer 1 went. Do not carry it in.

**The gap between calls (even if it is 2 minutes)**
- Stand up. Water.
- Jot one line: what did Interviewer 1 care about most?
- Re-read your hook and your two stories for Interviewer 2.
- Smile before you click in.

**Day before**
- If you have the interviewers' names, look them up on LinkedIn and send a connection request: "Hi [Name], looking forward to speaking with you on Monday regarding the Marketing Leadership Development Program. Best, Alex"
- Test Zoom, camera at eye level, light in front of you, clean background.

## Section 7. Marketing Thinking Questions

These test how you think, not whether you know the "right" answer. Structure beats speed: say your framework out loud, then walk it.

**A simple frame for any "how would you market X" question**
1. Who is the customer? (segment)
2. What do they need, and what stops them today?
3. What is the message?
4. Where do you reach them? (channel)
5. How do you know it worked? (the metric)

**Practice questions**
1. How would you market a Chase credit card to college students? `FIELD (long)`
2. How would you measure whether a campaign worked? `FIELD (long)`
3. Tell me about a brand or campaign you admire. What makes it work? `FIELD (long)`
4. Tell me about a JPMorganChase or Chase campaign you've noticed. What would you change? `FIELD (long)`
5. Walk me through your Dutch Bros analysis. How did you size the opportunity and compare scenarios? `FIELD (long)`
6. What is CPM, and why did you use it? `FIELD (long)`
