## Section 1. What This Call Is (3 minutes)

A recruiter found you on LinkedIn and reached out. You didn't apply, so something on your profile caught their eye. This call has two goals:

1. **Learn** enough about the role and company to decide if you want it.
2. **Make it easy** for the recruiter to put you in front of the hiring manager.

You don't need to be an expert on leasing. You need to be curious, clear, and easy to talk to.

## Section 5. Questions to Ask (6 minutes)

This is where you learn if you want the job. React to each answer before asking the next one.

`STARTER`
1. What specifically about my background caught your attention?
2. Can you tell me a little more about the team, and why this role is open right now?
3. Is there a formal job description you could send me?
4. What systems does the team use day to day, like a lease management system?
5. What do the hours look like? Is it pretty much 9 to 5, or does it change depending on what's going on?
6. What's the pay range for the role?
7. What are the next steps in the process?

## Section 6. Pay (2 minutes)

Pay is fair game on a recruiter call. Ask it yourself (Question 6): "What's the pay range for the role?"

If the range works: "That works for me."

If they turn it back to you: "I'd want to be paid fairly for the market, but getting the right experience matters just as much to me right now."
