About the job

Application Deadline: 11/30/2026

Address: 151 W 42nd Street

Job Family Group: Capital Markets Sales & Service

BMO Capital Markets is seeking an Analyst to join the Global Markets Corporate
Banking team in New York. This is an entry-level analyst position supporting
coverage bankers on credit origination, portfolio monitoring and transaction
execution for large corporate clients.

What You Will Do

- Build and maintain financial models, including credit and cash flow models,
  to support underwriting and ongoing portfolio monitoring.
- Prepare credit approval memoranda and internal presentations summarizing
  transaction structure, risk considerations and client relationship context.
- Conduct industry and company research, including comparable company analysis
  and analysis of financial statements.
- Support deal execution across revolving credit facilities, term loans and
  related capital markets products.
- Coordinate with risk, legal, operations and product partners to move
  transactions through internal approval processes.
- Maintain accurate pipeline and portfolio data, and contribute to quarterly
  portfolio reviews.

Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, economics or a related discipline.
- 0-2 years of relevant experience in corporate banking, credit, capital
  markets or a related field. Recent graduates are encouraged to apply.
- Strong financial modeling and analytical skills, including advanced Excel.
- Working knowledge of accounting and financial statement analysis.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to
  prepare clear credit memoranda.
- Detail oriented, organized, and able to manage multiple deadlines.
- Interest in corporate banking and the capital markets.
